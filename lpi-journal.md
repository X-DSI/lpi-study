**Objectives touched:101.1**
**Time:1 hour**

### What I learned

- There needs to be a standard for instruction sets and device communication
- Until mid 2000s, it was BIOS (Basic Input/Output System)
- since the first decade of 2000s, it has been UEFI (Unified Extensible Firmware Interface)
- Both are used interchangeble since they fulfill the same basic purpose
- Devices (PCs) have a device activation key assigned by the manufacturers like F2 or F12 or Del.
- Two ways to identify hardware resources in Linux
  - to use specialized Commands
  - to read specific files inside special filesystems
-
